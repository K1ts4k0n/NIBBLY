# Product photo credits

The website product photos are local copies of images downloaded from Unsplash and used under the Unsplash License. They are deliberately mapped to the related snack types (granola, berries, energy bites, dried fruit, nuts, matcha, cookies, and protein snacks).

Sources:

- Granola and berry food photography: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-bowl-of-granola-with-raspberries-and-nuts-Ckb10FIdslw)
- All remaining product imagery: selected from the relevant Unsplash food photography search collections, including granola, energy balls, banana chips, dried mango, oatmeal cookies, nuts, matcha, protein balls, cocoa nuts, coconut granola, apple cinnamon granola, pumpkin seeds, and strawberry yogurt.

Each local image filename corresponds to the product mock-data `art` identifier in `data/products.ts`.
